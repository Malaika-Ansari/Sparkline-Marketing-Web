## 1. Introduction

Sparkline Marketing includes **11 pre-architected report templates** designed for diverse business stakeholders: C-suite executives, cross-functional marketing teams, external agency clients, and potential investors.

Each template features a targeted curation of modular sections, optimized layout density, and custom narrative emphasis. Users can generate reports directly from these templates or use them as baselines in the **5-Step Custom Report Builder**.

## 3. Detailed Template Profiles

### 1. Executive Marketing Strategy
- **Template ID**: `executive_strategy`
- **Primary Use Case**: Quarterly board reviews, venture capital pitch updates, and executive strategic alignment.
- **Tone**: High-level, macro-commercial, deterministic, ROI-focused.
- **Key Modules Highlighted**:
  - Growth objectives and CAC guardrails.
  - Recommended marketing budget by high-level channel group.
  - 12-month balanced revenue and break-even timeline.
  - Phased 30/60/90-day execution milestones.

### 2. Complete Marketing Plan
- **Template ID**: `complete_plan`
- **Primary Use Case**: Comprehensive annual or semi-annual operating manual for internal marketing departments and cross-functional partners.
- **Tone**: Tactical, rigorous, structured, execution-oriented.
- **Key Modules Highlighted**:
  - Full company background, products/services pricing and unit margins.
  - Multi-channel paid acquisition flight structures.
  - Organic content clustering and editorial publishing cadence.
  - Technical SEO requirements and conversion rate optimization actions.

### 3. Market Research Report
- **Template ID**: `market_research`
- **Primary Use Case**: Market expansion feasibility, new product category launches, and TAM/SAM addressability validation.
- **Tone**: Analytical, macroeconomic, evidence-backed.
- **Key Modules Highlighted**:
  - Total Addressable Market (TAM) and Serviceable Addressable Market (SAM).
  - PESTLE environmental scan (regulations, tech adoption, demographic shifts).
  - Consumer buying behavior and demand friction analysis.

### 4. Competitor Analysis Report
- **Template ID**: `competitor_analysis`
- **Primary Use Case**: Competitive benchmarking, product positioning workshops, and counter-positioning campaigns.
- **Tone**: Objective, comparative, diagnostic.
- **Key Modules Highlighted**:
  - Feature matrix comparing core offerings vs. key direct competitors.
  - Competitor pricing models, discounts, and enterprise contract terms.
  - Identification of competitor weak points (e.g. slow support, legacy UI, vendor lock-in).
  - Perceptual value positioning map.

### 5. SWOT & PESTLE Strategic Synthesis
- **Template ID**: `swot_pestle`
- **Primary Use Case**: Corporate strategic planning retreats, risk audits, and organizational resilience reviews.
- **Tone**: Structured, governance-oriented, defensive and offensive.
- **Key Modules Highlighted**:
  - 4-quadrant SWOT matrix with strategic cross-leverage recommendations.
  - PESTLE analysis evaluating geopolitical, fiscal, and regulatory factors.
  - Risk assessment with probability, impact scoring, and mitigation playbooks.

### 6. Marketing Budget & Financial Report
- **Template ID**: `budget_financial`
- **Primary Use Case**: Capital allocation approval from finance committees and CFOs.
- **Tone**: Econometric, quantitative, margin-conscious.
- **Key Modules Highlighted**:
  - Channel budget distributions (Paid Search, Paid Social, Creative, SEO, Buffer).
  - Multi-currency valuations across USD, EUR, GBP, CAD, AUD, and JPY.
  - Unit economics: Blended CAC, Customer Lifetime Value (LTV), LTV:CAC ratio.
  - Payback period and sensitivity modeling.

### 7. Advertising Campaign Report
- **Template ID**: `campaign_report`
- **Primary Use Case**: Client performance updates for digital advertising agencies and in-house growth teams.
- **Tone**: Performance-focused, conversion-centric, creative-driven.
- **Key Modules Highlighted**:
  - Platform flight schedule across Meta Ads, Google Ads, and LinkedIn Ads.
  - Creative asset specifications (aspect ratios, video lengths, messaging angles).
  - Target pacing and campaign daily budget limits.

### 8. SEO Audit Report
- **Template ID**: `seo_audit`
- **Primary Use Case**: Technical audits for engineering teams, webmasters, and organic growth consultants.
- **Tone**: Technical, diagnostic, prioritized by search impact.
- **Key Modules Highlighted**:
  - Technical crawl health, indexing status, and Core Web Vitals (LCP, FID, CLS).
  - Keyword targeting portfolio categorized by transactional and commercial intent.
  - On-page internal linking and site architecture improvements.

### 9. Social Media & Content Strategy
- **Template ID**: `content_strategy`
- **Primary Use Case**: Onboarding content creators, social media managers, and brand strategists.
- **Tone**: Brand-building, engaging, narrative-driven.
- **Key Modules Highlighted**:
  - Content pillar architecture (Pillars, Clusters, Educational vs. Promotional splits).
  - Channel playbooks for LinkedIn, Instagram, YouTube, and X.
  - Messaging hooks, tone of voice rules, and editorial workflow schedules.

### 10. Marketing Forecast & ROI Report
- **Template ID**: `forecast_roi`
- **Primary Use Case**: Growth capital fundraising, investor briefings, and executive performance forecasting.
- **Tone**: Modeled, scenario-based, probabilistic.
- **Key Modules Highlighted**:
  - Three distinct growth scenarios: Conservative (base-case), Balanced (target), Optimistic (upside).
  - Break-even acquisition milestones and cumulative margin forecasts.
  - Risk sensitivity analysis highlighting key operational vulnerabilities.

### 11. Complete Marketing Project Report
- **Template ID**: `complete_project`
- **Primary Use Case**: M&A due diligence packages, agency master deliverables, and enterprise archival dossiers.
- **Tone**: Comprehensive, encyclopedic, authoritative.
- **Key Modules Highlighted**:
  - Full execution of all 25 modular sections in strict logical hierarchy.
  - Complete data provenance citations across every metric and qualitative claim.

## 4. Customizing Templates in the Builder

Every template serves as a starting point. Through the **Custom Builder Wizard**:
1. **Select or Deselect Sections**: Toggle any of the 25 sections on or off.
2. **Reorder Sections**: Organize sections into custom presentation sequences.
3. **Select Detail Level**:
   - **Brief**: High-level summaries, core KPIs, and top 3 recommendations only (~4–6 pages).
   - **Standard**: Full narrative analysis, structured tables, and comprehensive recommendations (~8–14 pages).
   - **Comprehensive**: In-depth explanations, secondary data tables, scenario breakdowns, and complete citation trails (~16–28 pages).
4. **Customize Branding**: Set report title, subtitle, prepared for, prepared by, custom report date, cover accent color, and confidentiality classification.
