# Content Generation Framework

The **Content Generation Framework** in Sparkline Marketing is built on strategic copywriting psychology, platform cultural norms, and rigorous anti-hallucination standards.

---

## 1. Content Generation Taxonomy

Content is classified across **11 core strategic formats**:

```
+---------------------+--------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| Content Format      | Primary Objective & Strategic Mechanism                                  |
+---------------------+--------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| Educational         | High-save breakdowns, frameworks, masterclasses, and tactical how-tos    |
| Promotional         | Time-sensitive launches, VIP waitlists, feature rollouts, and offers     |
| Informational       | Industry benchmark data, survey results, research summaries, and news    |
| Storytelling        | Founder candid journeys, operational lessons, case studies, and epiphanies|
| Problem-Solution    | Friction callout, diagnosis of traditional methods, and clean solution   |
| Product Showcase    | In-depth workflow demos, UI screencasts, feature highlights, and walkthroughs|
| Behind-the-Scenes   | Lab testing, team sprints, architectural sketches, and culture unboxing  |
| Customer Education  | Addressing common objections, onboarding tips, FAQs, and best practices  |
| Brand Awareness     | Contrarian points of view, cultural commentary, and brand positioning    |
| Engagement Post     | Strategic community polls, open questions, and debate prompts            |
| Announcement        | Major product releases, milestones, event appearances, and partnerships  |
+---------------------+--------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

---

## 2. Anatomy of High-Converting Social Copy

Every post generated by the studio contains 5 structured components:

1. **The Scroll-Stopping Hook**:
   - The first 1–2 lines determine whether the reader pauses or scrolls past.
   - *Techniques*: Contrarian statements, question hooks, quantified results, and curiosity gaps.
2. **The Core Message (Body)**:
   - Delivers immediate value without fluff.
   - Uses clear line breaks, bullet points, and visual spacing to enhance readability on mobile devices.
3. **The Call-to-Action (CTA)**:
   - Single, unambiguous next step aligned with the post objective.
   - *Examples*: "Save this for your next sprint", "Drop your perspective below", "Tap link in bio to test free".
4. **Platform-Tailored Hashtags**:
   - Curated into Broad, Niche, Product, Audience, and Campaign clusters.
5. **Visual Direction for Creators**:
   - Provides concrete creative guidance for video editors, graphic designers, or presenters (e.g. "Direct-to-camera close-up with dynamic animated text overlay").

---

## 3. Epistemic Guardrails (Anti-Hallucination Policy)

To preserve brand credibility and ethical marketing standards:
- **Zero Fabricated Testimonials**: The AI assistant will never generate fake quotes attributed to imaginary customers.
- **No Unsupported Certifications**: Never claims FDA approval, ISO certification, or proprietary awards unless verified in the user's project profile.
- **No Artificial Discounts**: Never fabricates phantom discount percentages or flash sales not authorized by the brand.
- **Clear Hypothesis Flagging**: When translating business features into audience benefits, assumptions are clearly labeled.
