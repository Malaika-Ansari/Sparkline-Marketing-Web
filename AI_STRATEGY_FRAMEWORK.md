# Sparkline Marketing — AI Strategy Framework

This document specifies the multi-dimensional strategic framework utilized by the **AI Marketing Strategist Engine** (Phase 07) to synthesize business parameters into actionable marketing intelligence.

---

## 1. Multi-Dimensional Synthesis Framework

The AI Strategist evaluates businesses across 8 core dimensions to prevent generic advice and deliver high-impact marketing strategy:

```
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                              8-DIMENSIONAL ANALYSIS FRAMEWORK                           |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|  A. Business Understanding  --> Model, industry, stage, strengths, operational bounds  |
|  B. Product Analysis        --> Features vs. benefits, USP, pricing, objections, PMF    |
|  C. Audience Intelligence   --> Verified user facts vs. AI behavioral hypotheses        |
|  D. Objectives & KPIs       --> Primary North Star, secondary metrics, time horizons    |
|  E. Channel Evaluation      --> Suitability, funnel role, pros/cons, resources, KPIs    |
|  F. Messaging Architecture  --> Brand positioning, core message, 3 angles, hooks, CTAs |
|  G. 6-Stage Funnel          --> Awareness, Interest, Consideration, Conversion,         |
|                                 Retention, Advocacy                                     |
|  H. Risks & Assumptions     --> Explicit assumptions, empirical research needs          |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

---

## 2. Dimension Specifications

### Dimension A: Business Understanding
- **Business Model**: Evaluates transactional dynamics (`B2B`, `B2C`, `D2C`, `Marketplace`, `Local Business`, `SaaS`).
- **Industry Context**: Evaluates category growth rate, maturity, and seasonal dynamics.
- **Stage Analysis**: Tailors risk tolerance based on `New (Pre-launch)`, `Growing`, `Established`, or `Rebranding`.
- **Operational Boundaries**: Respects business scale (`Solo`, `Small`, `Medium`, `Enterprise`).

### Dimension B: Product Analysis & Unit Economics
- **Features vs. Benefits Separation**: Systematically translates technical specifications (e.g., *"20% L-Ascorbic Acid stabilized with ferulic acid"*) into tangible customer outcomes (e.g., *"Eliminates dark spots without serum turning yellow on skin"*).
- **Unique Selling Proposition (USP)**: Identifies the single most compelling reason to buy versus existing substitutes.
- **Gross Margin & Pricing Power**: Computes gross margin percentage `(Price - COGS) / Price` to establish sustainable Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) ceilings.

### Dimension C: Target Audience Psychology
- **Transparency Separation**: Explicitly labels **User-Provided Facts** (baseline customer data from project onboarding) from **AI Strategic Hypotheses** (inferred psychological triggers and behavioral tendencies requiring validation).
- **Friction Points & Objections**: Direct cataloging of reasons prospects hesitate or abandon carts.
- **Purchasing Behavior**: Adapts strategy for `Impulse`, `Research-heavy`, `Value-driven`, `Luxury/Premium`, or `Subscription` buyer journeys.

### Dimension D: Marketing Objectives & Measurement
- **Primary North Star**: Direct calibration around the user's primary selected metric (e.g., *Increase sales*, *Generate qualified leads*, *Improve ROAS*).
- **Secondary KPIs**: Funnel health metrics (CTR, CVR, AOV, CPA, LTV:CAC, Churn).
- **Time Horizon**: Realistic planning increments (`1-3 months`, `3-6 months`, `6-12 months`, `Ongoing`).

### Dimension E: Channel Recommendation & Evaluation Matrix
Channels are never recommended blindly. Each candidate channel is evaluated across:
1. **Suitability Rationale**: Why this channel works for the specific product and audience.
2. **Funnel Role**: Which stage it powers (`Awareness`, `Consideration`, `Conversion`, `Retention`).
3. **Budget Share**: Percentage allocation of the user's monthly budget envelope.
4. **Advantages (Pros)**: Commercial leverage points.
5. **Limitations & Risks (Cons)**: Creative burnout, CPM volatility, tracking decay.
6. **Resource Requirements**: `Low`, `Medium`, or `High` effort.
7. **Suggested KPIs**: Target CPA, CTR, ROAS, and conversion benchmarks.

### Dimension F: Messaging Architecture
- **Brand Positioning**: Clear positioning statement relative to category incumbents.
- **Core Marketing Message**: Single memorable brand hook.
- **Three Supporting Angles**:
  - *Angle 1: Proof & Stability / Technical Superiority*
  - *Angle 2: Barrier Relief / Ease & Accessibility*
  - *Angle 3: Fair Value / Disruption of Legacy Markup*
- **Call-to-Action (CTA) Library**: Actionable buttons tailored for cold vs. warm audiences.
- **Brand Tone**: Voice, personality, and vocabulary guidance.

### Dimension G: 6-Stage Full Funnel Strategy
1. **Awareness**: Top-of-funnel reach, hook-driven video ads, problem-aware education.
2. **Interest**: Mid-funnel engagement, quizzes, comparison teardowns, architectural whitepapers.
3. **Consideration**: Overcoming objections, social proof, dermatologist reviews, trust badges.
4. **Conversion**: Frictionless checkout, risk reversal (30-day money-back guarantee), cart recovery.
5. **Retention**: Automated email/SMS onboarding flows, replenishment subscriptions, customer success.
6. **Advocacy**: Referral rewards, user-generated content (UGC) incentives, community advocacy.

### Dimension H: Risks, Assumptions & Empirical Research
- **Explicit Assumptions**: Lists all operational assumptions made by the model.
- **Areas Requiring External Research**: Highlights specific market factors that cannot be inferred by AI and must be validated through ad auction tests, pricing surveys, or customer interviews.
- **Commercial Reality Disclaimer**: Explicitly states that revenue forecasts are indicative planning benchmarks rather than contractual guarantees.

## 3. The Three Strategic Horizons

To provide flexibility across varying capital risk profiles, the engine generates three distinct strategic approaches:

| Dimension | Conservative Strategy | Balanced Strategy (Recommended) | Aggressive Growth Strategy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Risk Level** | Low | Moderate | High |
| **Primary Focus** | Bottom-funnel demand capture | Blended paid acquisition & retention | Rapid market share capture & broad testing |
| **Budget Split** | 65-70% High-Intent Search, 20% Retention, 15% Organic | 40% Paid Social, 35% Search, 15% Lifecycle, 10% Testing | 65% Paid Social/Video, 20% Search, 15% Creator Blitz |
| **Trade-Off** | Preserves capital; slower scale | Optimal balance of cash flow & growth | Maximum growth velocity; higher creative fatigue |
| **Timeline** | 90-day demand validation cycle | 180-day growth playbook | 60-day rapid scale sprint |
