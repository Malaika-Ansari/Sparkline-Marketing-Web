## 1. The 7-Dimension Recommendation Matrix

Every marketing recommendation generated by Sparkline is evaluated against a 7-dimensional scoring rubric to prevent low-leverage "checklist marketing":

| Dimension | Evaluation Question | Scoring Scale | Weight |
|---|---|:---:|:---:|
| **1. Business Relevance** | Does this directly serve the business model and stated core objective? | 1–10 | 20% |
| **2. Audience Resonance** | Does this address the acute pain points identified in audience research? | 1–10 | 20% |
| **3. Evidence Strength** | Is this supported by verified research findings and competitor gaps? | 1–10 | 15% |
| **4. Strategic Leverage** | Does this create long-term compounding assets vs. temporary vanity metrics? | 1–10 | 15% |
| **5. Resource Efficiency** | Can this be executed within the stated monthly budget and team bandwidth? | 1–10 | 10% |
| **6. Execution Velocity** | How quickly can this be deployed and produce statistical learning feedback? | 1–10 | 10% |
| **7. Risk Profile** | What is the financial and reputational downside exposure if it fails? | 1–10 (Inverse) | 10% |

$$\text{Priority Score} = \sum_{d=1}^{7} (S_d \times W_d)$$

## 2. Growth Modes & Risk Calibrations

Marketers operate under vastly different capital and runway realities. Sparkline supports three explicit strategic horizons:

### A. Lean Growth (Organic-First & Creator Seeding)
- **Primary Persona**: Bootstrapped founders, early-stage ventures, pre-revenue concepts.
- **Resource Intensity**: Low capital ($1,500 – $3,500/mo); High internal founder/creator time.
- **Strategic Thesis**: Prioritize high-trust, low-CAC organic distribution: educational short-form video, micro-creator seeding, SEO editorial pillars, and community advocacy.
- **Key Metric**: Organic conversion rate and referral share.
- **Primary Risk**: Slower initial pipeline velocity; highly dependent on organic algorithm favorability.

### B. Balanced Growth (Integrated Paid & Retention Engine)
- **Primary Persona**: Growing businesses, funded startups, profitable eCommerce/SaaS brands.
- **Resource Intensity**: Moderate capital ($5,000 – $15,000/mo); Dedicated growth marketer or agency.
- **Strategic Thesis**: Balance scalable top-of-funnel paid media (Meta Ads, Google Search) with compounding bottom-of-funnel lifecycle retention (automated email flows, SMS replenishment).
- **Key Metric**: Blended ROAS (>3.0x) and 60-Day Repeat Purchase Rate (>35%).
- **Primary Risk**: Creative fatigue requires 3–4 new visual hooks weekly; rising ad auction CPMs.

### C. Accelerated Growth (Multi-Channel Category Scale)
- **Primary Persona**: Venture-backed scale-ups, established category challengers, retail expansion brands.
- **Resource Intensity**: High capital ($25,000 – $60,000+/mo); Cross-functional growth department.
- **Strategic Thesis**: Aggressive multi-channel blitz across paid social, high-intent search, podcast host reads, influencer PR, and retail co-selling to capture category dominance.
- **Key Metric**: Net New ARR velocity, Market Share, and Blended CAC payback period (<12 months).
- **Primary Risk**: High cash burn if conversion rates dip; inventory stockout exposure.

## 3. Separation of Fact, Assumption, and Recommendation

Sparkline adheres to rigorous epistemic honesty:

1. **Verified Facts**:
   - Explicitly provided by user in Project Profile (e.g., product selling price of $58, stated budget of $8,000/mo).
   - Documented competitor pricing from public storefronts or third-party retailer listings.

2. **Calibrated Deductions**:
   - Synthesized from audience pain points and category benchmarks (e.g., 45-day replenishment repurchase cycle for 30ml serum).
   - Tagged with confidence rating (`High`, `Medium`, `Low`, `Insufficient Evidence`).

3. **Operational Assumptions**:
   - Explicitly listed in the **Audit Card** and **Risks & Evidence Panel**.
   - Example: *"Assumes baseline eCommerce conversion rate of 2.8% based on modern Shopify storefront standards."*

4. **Prescriptive Recommendations**:
   - Clearly separated into time-bound tasks with assigned roles, dependencies, and required resources.
