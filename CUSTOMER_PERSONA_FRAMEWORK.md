# Sparkline Marketing — Customer Persona Framework

This document specifies the structural standards, psychological dimensions, and ethical rules governing **Customer Personas** in Sparkline Marketing (Phase 08).

---

## 1. Ethical Standards & Anti-Fabrication Principles

To ensure truthfulness, compliance, and actionable marketing utility, Sparkline Marketing establishes four non-negotiable rules for persona generation:

1. **Explicit Fictional Status**: Every customer persona is explicitly flagged with `isFictional: true` and labeled in the user interface as a *"Fictional Archetype Representation"*.
2. **Never Present Personas as Real People**: The engine never generates fake social profiles, fake home addresses, or fake photos that simulate actual individuals.
3. **Transparent Demographic Assumptions**: Age ranges and geographic clusters are explicitly marked as *assumptions* based on buying power and category trends unless confirmed by first-party user data.
4. **No Artificial Survey Statistics**: The platform never fabricates numerical survey claims (e.g. *"82% of mothers in Ohio prefer this packaging"*) without verified empirical research data.

---

## 2. The Four Pillars of Customer Persona Architecture

Each persona is modeled across four comprehensive dimensions:

```
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                               FOUR PERSONA DIMENSIONS                                   |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|  1. Identity & Characteristics  --> Archetype name, segment, age assumption, role, context |
|  2. Customer Psychology         --> Goals, needs, frustrations, pain points, objections |
|  3. Marketing Behavior          --> Platforms, content habits, purchase triggers, style |
|  4. Marketing Recommendations   --> Best messaging angle, content formats, CTAs, benefits |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

### Pillar 1: Identity & Characteristics
- **Persona Name**: Fictional character archetype (e.g. *Elena Ramos (The Ingredient Skeptic)*).
- **Segment Association**: The parent audience segment it represents.
- **Tagline**: Single-sentence summary of the persona's core posture.
- **Age Range Assumption**: Explicitly labeled assumption (e.g. *31 - 38 years old (Estimated assumption based on discretionary spend)*).
- **Geographic Context**: Market environment (e.g. *Urban Tech Metros*).
- **Occupation / Role**: Professional or life role.
- **Interests**: Specific hobby, intellectual, or category affinities.

### Pillar 2: Customer Psychology
- **Goals**: What the persona seeks to achieve in life or work.
- **Needs**: Functional requirements the product must fulfill.
- **Frustrations**: Irritations with existing alternatives or poor legacy experiences.
- **Pain Points**: Acute commercial or physical friction.
- **Motivations**: Underlying emotional, status, or financial drivers.
- **Buying Objections**: Specific reasons the persona hesitates or walks away.
- **Decision Factors**: Critical criteria evaluated at the point of purchase.

### Pillar 3: Marketing Behavior
- **Preferred Platforms**: Where the persona actually spends digital attention.
- **Content Preferences**: Video length, written depth, interactive formats.
- **Purchase Triggers**: The catalytic event that moves them from passive observer to active buyer.
- **Buying Journey Overview**: Narrative summary of their multi-touch path to conversion.
- **Communication Style**: Preferred tone of voice (e.g. *scientific, empathetic, humorous, direct*).

### Pillar 4: Tactical Marketing Recommendations
- **Best Messaging Angle**: Primary headline hook designed to resonate with their specific pain point.
- **Recommended Formats**: Exact creative formats that break through their skepticism.
- **Suitable Channels**: Specific ad platforms and inbound channels.
- **Suggested Call-to-Action (CTA)**: Frictionless button copy.
- **Relevant Product Benefits**: Specific features from the user's product catalog that matter most to this persona.
