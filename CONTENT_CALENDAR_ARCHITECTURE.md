# Content Calendar & Scheduling Architecture

The **Content Calendar** in Sparkline Marketing provides an interactive planning and scheduling interface to organize multi-platform content pipelines.

---

## 1. Calendar View Modes & State Management

```
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                                    Content Calendar                                     |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
       |                                                               |
       v                                                               v
[ Monthly View ]                                                [ List View ]
  - 7x5 Day Grid                                                  - Chronological Pipeline
  - Date Jump & Navigation                                        - Quick Status Toggles
  - Click Day Cell -> Open Modal on that date                     - Platform Badges
  - Color-Coded Scheduled Items                                   - Performance Indicators
```

### Supported Status Lifecycle
1. **Idea**: Conceptual topic recorded in the studio or generated by the idea brainstormer.
2. **Draft**: Copy, hooks, and creative assets under development.
3. **Ready for Review**: Formatted draft awaiting team or client review.
4. **Approved**: Verified by stakeholders; ready for scheduled publication.
5. **Scheduled**: Date and time assigned in the editorial calendar.
6. **Published**: Live asset with performance tracking enabled.
7. **Archived**: Past campaign record retained for audit history.

---

## 2. Date Handling & Timezone Safety

- **Local Timezone Formatting**: Calendar dates are rendered relative to the user's browser local time (`Intl.DateTimeFormat`) without timezone shift distortion.
- **ISO-8601 Compliance**: Storage in LocalStorage adheres strictly to `YYYY-MM-DD` and `HH:mm` format.
- **Flight Coordination**: Allows linking scheduled content items directly to active campaign IDs and marketing project records.
