# Marketing KPI & Measurement Framework
## Sparkline Marketing — Phase 11 Metrics Catalog

> **"You cannot scale what you do not measure."**  
> *Standard mathematical formulas, strategic rationales, and benchmark targets for modern marketing organizations.*

---

## 1. Metrics Catalog Overview

Sparkline categorizes performance indicators into five commercial tiers:
1. **Acquisition**: Efficiency of converting prospects into paying customers.
2. **Revenue**: Top-line media yield and marketing efficiency ratios.
3. **Retention**: Cohort repurchase velocity and customer lifetime value.
4. **Awareness**: Top-of-funnel reach, share of voice, and brand impressions.
5. **Engagement**: Micro-conversion velocity, click-through rates, and video completion.

---

## 2. Core Metrics Matrix

### 1. Blended Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)
- **Category**: Acquisition
- **Formula**:
  $$\text{Blended CAC} = \frac{\text{Total Sales \& Marketing Spend}}{\text{Total New Paying Customers Acquired}}$$
- **Strategic Rationale**: Guarantees scalable unit economics as paid ad channels expand across Meta, Google, and TikTok. Prevents unprofitable customer acquisition.
- **Data Required**: Ad platform spend (Meta, Google) + eCommerce/CRM new customer orders.
- **Measurement Frequency**: Weekly
- **Benchmark Range**: Target $< 40\%$ of first-order AOV for DTC; $< 14$ months payback for B2B SaaS.

---

### 2. Blended Return on Ad Spend (Blended ROAS / MER)
- **Category**: Revenue
- **Formula**:
  $$\text{Blended ROAS} = \frac{\text{Total Net Store Revenue}}{\text{Total Advertising Spend Across All Channels}}$$
- **Strategic Rationale**: Measures the aggregate commercial efficiency of top-of-funnel paid media investments without falling prey to platform-specific attribution inflation.
- **Data Required**: Total net revenue divided by total paid ad spend across all active channels.
- **Measurement Frequency**: Weekly
- **Benchmark Range**: Target $> 3.2\times$ during balanced growth phase; $> 2.4\times$ during aggressive scale.

---

### 3. 60-Day Customer Repeat Purchase Rate
- **Category**: Retention
- **Formula**:
  $$\text{Repeat Rate}_{60\text{d}} = \left(\frac{\text{Customers with } \ge 2\text{ Purchases within 60 Days}}{\text{Total First-Time Customers in Cohort}}\right) \times 100$$
- **Strategic Rationale**: In consumer goods and consumables, long-term enterprise valuation depends on repeat purchase velocity rather than first-order profitability.
- **Data Required**: eCommerce cohort data (Shopify, WooCommerce, custom database).
- **Measurement Frequency**: Monthly (Cohorted)
- **Benchmark Range**: Target $> 35\% - 42\%$ for skincare and consumables.

---

### 4. Active Subscription Penetration Share
- **Category**: Retention
- **Formula**:
  $$\text{Subscription Share} = \left(\frac{\text{Active Auto-Replenishment Subscribers}}{\text{Total Active Customer Base}}\right) \times 100$$
- **Strategic Rationale**: Creates predictable Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR), shields against ad auction volatility, and extends Customer Lifetime Value (LTV).
- **Data Required**: Recharge, Bold, or Shopify Subscriptions active subscriber database.
- **Measurement Frequency**: Monthly
- **Benchmark Range**: Target $> 25\% - 30\%$ of total customer orders.

---

### 5. Owned Channel (Email & SMS) Revenue Share
- **Category**: Revenue
- **Formula**:
  $$\text{Owned Revenue Share} = \left(\frac{\text{Net Revenue Attributed to Email \& SMS}}{\text{Total Net Store Revenue}}\right) \times 100$$
- **Strategic Rationale**: High owned-channel revenue cushions against digital ad platform price inflation and algorithm updates.
- **Data Required**: Klaviyo, Attentive, or Omnisend last-click attributed revenue reports.
- **Measurement Frequency**: Monthly
- **Benchmark Range**: Target $25\% - 32\%$ of total monthly store revenue.

---

### 6. Storefront Conversion Rate (CVR)
- **Category**: Acquisition
- **Formula**:
  $$\text{CVR} = \left(\frac{\text{Total Completed Transactions}}{\text{Total Unique Website Sessions}}\right) \times 100$$
- **Strategic Rationale**: Directly dictates paid media efficiency. A 30% increase in CVR lowers blended CAC by roughly 23% across all paid traffic sources.
- **Data Required**: Google Analytics 4 sessions and order transaction events.
- **Measurement Frequency**: Weekly
- **Benchmark Range**: Target $> 3.2\%$ on desktop; $> 2.4\%$ on mobile devices.

---

### 7. Net Revenue Retention (NRR) — B2B SaaS
- **Category**: Retention
- **Formula**:
  $$\text{NRR} = \left(\frac{\text{Starting ARR} + \text{Expansion ARR} - \text{Contraction ARR} - \text{Churn ARR}}{\text{Starting ARR}}\right) \times 100$$
- **Strategic Rationale**: The single most powerful valuation multiple driver in B2B enterprise SaaS, measuring compound account expansion.
- **Data Required**: Billing system cohort renewal and upsell contract values.
- **Measurement Frequency**: Quarterly
- **Benchmark Range**: Target $> 115\% - 125\%$ for top-quartile enterprise software.

---

## 3. Strict Measurement Principles

1. **Zero Fake Metrics**: Sparkline never displays fabricated real-time revenue numbers or fake ad spend dashboards unless connected to verified user data.
2. **Formula Transparency**: Every KPI clearly exposes its mathematical definition so cross-functional teams share identical accounting rules.
3. **Blended Over Platform Attribution**: Encourages blended marketing efficiency ratio (MER) tracking to avoid Meta and Google double-counting conversion credits.
