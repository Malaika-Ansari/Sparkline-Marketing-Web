# Brand Voice & Content Consistency Guidelines

The **Brand Voice Calibration Engine** in Sparkline Marketing enables marketing teams and agencies to establish a persistent, project-linked brand tone that governs all AI copywriting, caption rewriting, and content generation.

---

## 1. Supported Brand Tones

| Tone Category | Characteristics | Optimal Channels | Sample Sentence |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Professional** | Authoritative, measured, data-backed, clear | LinkedIn, YouTube, Case Studies | "We analyze campaign efficiency through verifiable unit economics and deterministic break-even models." |
| **Friendly** | Warm, encouraging, approachable, helpful | Instagram, Email, Community | "We're here to help you make sense of your ad spend without the stress of messy spreadsheets." |
| **Conversational** | Natural, authentic, direct, relatable | X (Twitter), Facebook, TikTok | "Let's be honest: burning ad budget on vanity impressions hurts. Here is how to fix it." |
| **Educational** | Instructive, analytical, clear, tactical | Blog, Carousels, Video Tutorials | "In this breakdown, we examine the mathematical relationship between gross margins and break-even ROAS." |
| **Bold** | Contrarian, decisive, high-energy, ambitious | Product Launches, TikTok, Ads | "Stop guessing your marketing spend. Scale what actually drives revenue and eliminate the rest." |
| **Luxury** | Refined, understated, elegant, aspirational | Pinterest, High-End DTC, Editorial | "Meticulously formulated for enduring radiance and quiet confidence." |
| **Minimal** | Direct, concise, zero-fluff, efficient | Developer Tools, SaaS, X | "Deterministic ROI calculations. Zero guesswork. Ready in 60 seconds." |
| **Playful** | Witty, creative, light-hearted, engaging | Reels, TikTok, Viral Shorts | "Spreadsheets called. They said they're tired of running your marketing campaigns." |

## 2. Reusable Voice Parameters

Each project's brand voice record stores:
- **Tone Selection**: The core emotional and intellectual baseline.
- **Preferred Vocabulary**: Essential domain phrases and terminology the brand embraces (e.g. `data-backed strategy`, `clear unit economics`, `verifiable growth`).
- **Words to Avoid**: Blacklisted buzzwords, spam triggers, and clichés (e.g. `guaranteed overnight riches`, `secret hack`, `insane results`, `miracle fix`).
- **Communication Guidelines**: Explicit strategic rules regarding company values, regulatory disclosures, and audience communication expectations.
- **Multilingual Support**: Tone consistency calibrated across **English**, **Urdu** (Nastaliq script), and **Roman Urdu**.
