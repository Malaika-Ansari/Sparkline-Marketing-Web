# Sparkline Marketing Cost Metrics & Formulas

## Standardized Formula Specifications

In growth marketing, accurate performance measurement requires clear definitions of the capital deployed (Ad Spend vs. Total Marketing Investment) and safe handling of edge cases (zero clicks, zero impressions).

Sparkline Marketing standardizes eight foundational marketing formulas.

---

## 1. CPC — Cost Per Click

### Formula:
$$\text{CPC} = \frac{\text{Total Direct Ad Spend}}{\text{Total Verified Clicks}}$$

* **Numerator**: Direct media fees paid to advertising networks (e.g., Meta, Google, TikTok).
* **Denominator**: Clicks generated by ad creative.
* **Division by Zero Protection**: If $\text{Clicks} = 0$, CPC is mathematically undefined. The interface displays an informational notice and prevents NaN/Infinity.
* **Strategic Role**: Measures audience intrigue and bidding efficiency in auction environments. Does not account for landing page conversion quality or creative production costs.

---

## 2. CPM — Cost Per Thousand Impressions

### Formula:
$$\text{CPM} = \left(\frac{\text{Total Direct Ad Spend}}{\text{Total Verified Impressions}}\right) \times 1,000$$

* **Numerator**: Direct ad spend.
* **Denominator**: Number of ad views/impressions.
* **Division by Zero Protection**: If $\text{Impressions} = 0$, CPM is undefined.
* **Strategic Role**: Standard benchmark for media inventory pricing. Highly sensitive to audience targeting narrowness, country geography, and ad placement format.

---

## 3. CPA — Cost Per Acquisition

### Formula:
$$\text{CPA} = \frac{\text{Total Campaign Investment}}{\text{Total Paying Customers (or Actions)}}$$

* **Numerator**: Comprehensive campaign costs, including media ad spend, video hooks, copy assets, landing page software, and agency retainers.
* **Denominator**: Total verified new customers or commercial acquisitions.
* **Division by Zero Protection**: If $\text{Acquisitions} = 0$, CPA is undefined.
* **Strategic Role**: Essential unit economics metric. Must be benchmarked against Average Order Value (AOV) and Customer Lifetime Value (LTV) to determine business viability.

---

## 4. CPL — Cost Per Lead

### Formula:
$$\text{CPL} = \frac{\text{Total Marketing Cost}}{\text{Total Qualified Leads Captured}}$$

* **Numerator**: All capital allocated to lead generation campaigns.
* **Denominator**: Verified lead forms, demo submissions, or gated content signups.
* **Division by Zero Protection**: If $\text{Leads} = 0$, CPL is undefined.
* **Strategic Role**: Key indicator for B2B and high-ticket service funnels. Must be paired with Lead-to-Opportunity conversion velocity.

---

## 5. CTR — Click-Through Rate

### Formula:
$$\text{CTR} = \left(\frac{\text{Total Clicks}}{\text{Total Impressions}}\right) \times 100\%$$

* **Output Unit**: Percentage (`%`).
* **Division by Zero Protection**: If $\text{Impressions} = 0$, CTR is undefined.
* **Strategic Role**: Directly tests ad creative hook strength and audience relevance. High CTR with poor conversion indicates clickbait messaging or landing page friction.

---

## 6. Conversion Rate (CVR)

### Formula:
$$\text{Conversion Rate} = \left(\frac{\text{Conversions}}{\text{Clicks / Visitors}}\right) \times 100\%$$

* **Output Unit**: Percentage (`%`).
* **Division by Zero Protection**: If $\text{Clicks} = 0$, CVR is undefined.
* **Strategic Role**: Gauges offer strength, pricing friction, and landing page user experience.

---

## 7. ROAS — Return On Ad Spend

### Formula:
$$\text{ROAS} = \frac{\text{Gross Revenue Attributed to Ads}}{\text{Total Direct Ad Spend}}$$

* **Output Unit**: Direct multiplier ratio (e.g., `3.80x` or `3.80 : 1`).
* **Division by Zero Protection**: If $\text{Ad Spend} = 0$, ROAS is undefined.
* **Critical Distinction**: **ROAS is NOT net profit.** An e-commerce campaign achieving a 3.0x ROAS will lose money if product cost of goods sold (COGS), shipping, merchant processing, and overhead exceed 67% of revenue.

---

## 8. ROI — Return On Investment

### Formula:
$$\text{ROI} = \left(\frac{\text{Net Commercial Return} - \text{Total Marketing Investment}}{\text{Total Marketing Investment}}\right) \times 100\%$$

* **Output Unit**: Percentage (`%`).
* **Numerator**: Net financial gain (Total Return minus Total Investment).
* **Denominator**: Total marketing investment (media, creative, tools, software, contractor fees).
* **Division by Zero Protection**: If $\text{Investment} = 0$, ROI is undefined.
* **Critical Distinction**: ROI reflects true business profitability and bottom-line health, unlike top-line advertising ROAS.
